Why Spatium?

Fast, parallel scans

Rust engine + rayon parallel traversal scans a 10-million-file volume in under 60 seconds on a mid-range NVMe SSD.

NTFS truth

Allocated vs. logical size, sparse-file awareness, hard-link detection, reparse-point handling. Don't be fooled by phantom size.

History + diff

"What grew 18 GB since last week?" — SQLite-backed scan history with diff. Free tools have no memory.

Cleanup staging

Non-destructive "Add to Cleanup" staging area. Review, deselect, then commit to Recycle Bin. No accidental deletes.

Quick Wins

Algorithmically-surfaced high-impact low-effort cleanup candidates (caches, logs, stale downloads, large unused installers).

Power-user UX

Three-column shell, command palette (Ctrl+K), keyboard navigation, dark mode default, accessible contrast.
